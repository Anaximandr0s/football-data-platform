Architecture

Live (teal): 1 a match is converted to one row per second, 2 replayed into Kafka second by second, 3 read by the Live page about half a second later, 4 counted by Spark in 5-minute windows, 5 shown on the Live page. Stored (purple): 6 the bronze-writer keeps every message in Bronze; at the final whistle of a whole match Dagster builds 7 Silver and 8 Gold, each checked by Soda, and 9 the analysis app reads Gold. The arrows move while it happens; a picture opens the real page.

Start

Only a match played from kick-off to the final whistle is stored and rebuilt by Dagster. Any other start is a partial replay: live only. Full match fast plays the whole match in about 100 s, the way to show Dagster at work.

At most 2 replays at once, one per match. A card leaves 30 s after its replay ends (stopped, partial, or stored in Gold).
